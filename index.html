Blackthorn Operator

Zachary Stone is a highly skilled operator and squad leader for special operations within The Blackthorn Forces (BTF). His squad is often simply called "Zach's Team," and is a small team mainly consisting of operators Owen and Carl. It is rare for other operators or soldiers to follow Zach's Team into a mission; unlike all other squad leaders, Zachary reports directly to Valecentis, head of Blackthorn, for the orders for his squad. This means that Zach's Team is often sent on very special or secretive operations at the orders of Valecentis and nobody else.

About Zachary

Zachary is fairly compassionate, honest, and brave. He looks after and prioritizes his team, and operators Carl and Owen look up to and respect him. He shows often that he wants to be someone people can count on and trust, and wants to fight for what is right. He heavily looks to his orders for guidance, but will not hesitate to ignore orders if it does not line up with what he think is right. This has gotten him into trouble with previous commanders, but so far has had very little disagreement with the leadership of Valecentis.
