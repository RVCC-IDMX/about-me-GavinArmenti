Blackthorn Status

Zachary Stone (Zach for short) is a highly skilled operator and squad leader for special operations within The Blackthorn Forces (BTF). His squad is often simply referred to as "Zach's team," and is a small team mainly consisting of operators Owen and Carl. It is rare for other operators or soldiers to follow Zach's Team into a mission; unlike all other squad leaders, Zachary reports directly to Valecentis, head of Blackthorn, for the orders for his squad. This means that Zach's team is often sent on very special or secretive operations at the orders of Valecentis and nobody else, and any new recruits to this team must go through Valecentis.
Zach and his team are highly anticipated to be involved in the spearheading of The Redmark Forest (dubbed this because of its forested and dangerous nature causing a display of a forest with a red mark on maps), as Valecentis has shown great interest in its security.

About Zachary

Zachary is fairly compassionate, honest, and brave. He looks after and prioritizes his team, and operators Carl and Owen look up to and respect him. He displays often that he wants to be someone people can count on and trust, and wants to fight for what is right. He heavily looks to his orders for guidance, but will not hesitate to ignore orders if they do not line up with what he believes is right. This mindset has gotten him into some trouble with his previous commandering officers. However, he has had very little disagreement with the leadership of Valecentis.
